# J-ROC AI Supplier Qualification and Sample Approval

A supplier is not approved because it has a website, marketplace listing, sales representative or low quote. Qualification is specific to the legal entity, factory, product model, production site and intended sales market.

## Intake evidence
- Legal entity name, registration details, physical address and authorized representative.
- Actual manufacturing site address; distinguish factory from trading company or broker.
- Product model, revision, datasheet, bill of materials summary and available drawings.
- Country of origin and manufacturing process.
- Written permission to private-label the exact model and apply J-ROC AI marks.
- References and verifiable business history.
- Quality-system certificates, scope, issuer, validity and site coverage where claimed.
- Product-specific test reports and certificates, including model identifiers, factory, standard edition, test lab and current status.
- Warranty, returns, spare parts, complaint handling and recall cooperation.
- Minimum order quantity, sample cost, production lead time, Incoterms, shipping, duties and payment terms.
- Written no-substitution and change-notification commitment.

## Verification
1. Verify the legal entity independently.
2. Verify the factory and whether the contact is the manufacturer, authorized distributor or broker.
3. Confirm certification and test-report details with the issuing body or laboratory where possible.
4. Confirm that the evidence covers the exact model, variant, manufacturing site and intended US use.
5. Review supplier references, complaint history and support capability.
6. Record all evidence and its review date in the J-ROC AI supplier record.

## Sample approval
- Obtain production-intent samples, not only a marketing sample.
- Photograph labels, packaging, ports, connectors, included accessories and serial/model identifiers.
- Compare sample against frozen specification and bill of materials.
- Test advertised functions, rated operating limits, interoperability and failure behavior.
- Record results, defects, corrective actions and retest evidence.
- Approve or reject in writing. A sample approval does not replace required regulatory testing or certification.

## Commercial and intellectual-property controls
Do not sign a purchase order or pay a deposit before the Chairman approves the exact supplier, product, quantity, landed-cost estimate and total commitment. The agreement must state ownership/licensing of brand assets, design files, firmware, tooling, packaging, manuals and test procedures. Prohibit unauthorized overruns, seconds, substitutions and use of J-ROC AI marks outside the contract.

## Status definitions
- **Unverified:** lead only; no purchase authority.
- **In Review:** evidence collection and verification underway.
- **Verified:** business identity and claimed role checked; product is not yet approved.
- **Product Approved:** exact product, sample, evidence and commercial terms accepted.
- **Rejected / Suspended:** do not order or list.

## Mandatory decision record
Supplier, legal entity, factory, product model, revision, intended use, evidence reviewed, unresolved risks, approved quantity, landed cost, reviewer, date and next review date.

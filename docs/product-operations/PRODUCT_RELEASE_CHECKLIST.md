# J-ROC AI Product Release Checklist

A product cannot be published unless the relevant checklist is complete and the product registry records the approvals.

## Physical product release
- [ ] Product code, SKU, model, revision and variants assigned.
- [ ] Exact manufacturer, factory and country of origin recorded.
- [ ] Private-label permission or manufacturing agreement signed and reviewed.
- [ ] Approved production-intent sample and inspection report recorded.
- [ ] Bill of materials and critical components controlled.
- [ ] Product-specific US compliance assessment completed by qualified reviewer.
- [ ] Required test reports, certifications, registrations and mark-use permissions verified for exact model and factory.
- [ ] Product label, serial/lot traceability, warnings and packaging approved.
- [ ] Complete user manual, quick-start, installation, maintenance, troubleshooting and disposal information approved.
- [ ] Compatibility claims tested and limited to verified configurations.
- [ ] Warranty, returns, repair, spare parts and recall process defined.
- [ ] Unit economics include landed cost, freight, duties, payment fees, fulfillment, returns and warranty reserve.
- [ ] Inventory lot received, inspected and released; no sample or forecast quantity counted as stock.
- [ ] Final quality, compliance and Chairman release approvals recorded.

## Digital product release
- [ ] Product code, product name, version and supported platforms assigned.
- [ ] Ownership and all third-party commercial redistribution rights verified.
- [ ] Third-party license inventory and software bill of materials reviewed.
- [ ] Security review, dependency review, privacy/data-flow review and backup plan completed.
- [ ] Build artifact stored with SHA-256 checksum.
- [ ] Install, upgrade, downgrade/rollback, activation, deactivation and uninstall tested.
- [ ] Supported operating systems, browsers, hardware and minimum requirements documented.
- [ ] User guide, quick-start, troubleshooting, release notes and support route published.
- [ ] License, subscription, refund and privacy terms published.
- [ ] Payment webhook, entitlement grant, renewal, cancellation and refund behavior tested.
- [ ] Accessibility and customer support checks completed.
- [ ] Final release approval recorded.

## Store listing checks
- [ ] Real product images and accurate specifications.
- [ ] No invented reviews, ratings, certifications, stock or delivery promises.
- [ ] Correct condition category and fulfillment method.
- [ ] Price and margin calculation reviewed.
- [ ] Required documents visible or downloadable.
- [ ] Warranty, returns, shipping and support terms visible.
- [ ] Product is marked public only after all release gates pass.

## Stop-ship triggers
Stop publication and shipment for any safety complaint, certification mismatch, unauthorized component substitution, counterfeit concern, material specification change, data breach, security vulnerability, repeated defect, inaccurate compatibility claim or missing required document. Quarantine affected inventory and record the decision.

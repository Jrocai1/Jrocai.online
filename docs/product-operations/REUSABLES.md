- Full operating model, product families and launch phases: see `../JROC_AI_PRODUCT_OPERATIONS.md`.

## Reusable templates to maintain
1. Supplier intake and evidence log.
2. Product requirements and target-cost brief.
3. Model and compatibility matrix.
4. Sample inspection and defect report.
5. Compliance applicability and evidence matrix.
6. Product manual and safety-warning template.
7. Packaging and brand-label approval sheet.
8. Product cost and contribution-margin calculator.
9. Production purchase approval.
10. Inventory receiving and lot inspection.
11. Digital software bill of materials and license review.
12. Digital security and privacy review.
13. Release notes and version manifest.
14. Warranty, returns and recall record.
15. Customer complaint and corrective-action record.

All templates are controlled documents. Update their version and reviewer whenever the process changes.

# J-ROC AI Product Operations

**Owner:** J-ROC AI  
**Retail channel:** Jrocai.Online  
**Development and validation partner:** J-ROC AI Factory  
**Operating model:** Physical private-label manufacturing + original/custom physical products + owned digital products  
**Initial spending rule:** $0 commitment until the Chairman explicitly approves a specific supplier, sample, certification, subscription, tooling, or production purchase.

## Mission

Build a portfolio of products sold under the J-ROC AI brand. J-ROC AI owns its brand identity, product requirements, approved packaging, product documentation, customer experience, and product records. Manufacturers may produce approved physical products under written agreements. Digital products are developed and distributed by J-ROC AI or licensed only where third-party rights permit.

### Production models
- **Private label:** existing manufacturer design, with written permission to apply J-ROC AI marks and packaging.
- **Custom manufacturing:** manufacturer builds to J-ROC AI-controlled specifications.
- **Original design:** J-ROC AI controls the product requirements and design files; manufacturing is contracted.

### Physical product release gate
A product must remain **Draft / Not for Sale** until all applicable items are complete:
- Supplier identity and legal business details verified.
- Written authorization to private-label the exact model and use all marks.
- Manufacturer, factory location, country of origin, materials and bill of materials recorded.
- Exact model, revision, variants, accessories and compatibility matrix frozen.
- Production-intent sample received and inspected.
- Risk assessment and applicable US federal, state and local requirements identified.
- Required test reports, certifications, registrations and mark-use authorizations verified for the exact model and factory. A component certificate or similar-looking model is not sufficient.
- Label, warnings, packaging and claims reviewed.
- Complete instructions, setup, operation, maintenance, troubleshooting and disposal information prepared.
- Warranty, returns, spare parts and recall contacts defined.
- Unit economics verified: landed cost, freight, duties, fulfillment, payment fees, returns allowance, warranty reserve and contribution margin.
- Final quality approval recorded by an authorized J-ROC AI reviewer.

No product may claim UL, ETL, FCC, Qi, IP rating, waterproofing, medical status, safety approval, or other certification unless the exact product and configuration have valid supporting evidence and mark-use rights.

### Supplier contract requirements
Written agreements should cover: exact specifications and approved sample; no unapproved substitutions; change notification; inspection and rejection rights; defect thresholds and remedies; traceability and batch records; audit and test evidence; intellectual-property ownership and limited brand license; confidentiality; tooling and source-file ownership; no unauthorized overruns or factory seconds; labor and sourcing standards; country of origin; packaging/manual obligations; warranty support; recall cooperation; indemnity; delivery terms; and exit/transition assistance. Obtain legal review before signing.

## Jrocai.Online catalog rules

- Only products with status **Published**, documentation complete, quality approved and compliance review approved may appear for sale.
- Physical products must show new/private-label versus certified pre-owned condition clearly. Used-working products remain a separate condition category.
- Dropship items, if ever approved, must be clearly identified as supplier-fulfilled and must not be represented as J-ROC AI-manufactured.
- No outside marketplace sellers are enabled by this operation.
- Do not create fake stock, fake reviews, invented ratings, fabricated certifications, unsupported product photos, or unverified delivery promises.
- Digital downloads are delivered only after entitlement/payment verification.
- Inventory and order status must come from recorded events, not sample numbers.

## Pricing and margin policy

Pricing is a decision, not a guessed markup. Calculate contribution margin using actual landed cost and variable selling costs. Suggested internal screening targets (not guarantees): 40–50% gross margin for physical products and 75–90% gross margin for digital products before operating overhead. Reject or reprice items that fail the Chairman-approved minimum after returns, warranty, fulfillment and payment costs are included.

## Operating workflow

1. Product idea submitted to J-ROC AI Factory.
2. Market demand and competitive pricing researched.
3. Product requirements and target economics written.
4. Supplier or development route qualified.
5. Samples or working digital build evaluated.
6. Compliance, security, documentation and quality gates completed.
7. Chairman approves the exact production/release commitment.
8. Approved listing and real inventory/digital entitlement published to Jrocai.Online.
9. Orders, returns, support, defects and margin monitored.
10. Changes trigger revision review; material changes can require retesting and reapproval.

### Apparel technical pack and quality controls
Each garment requires an approved technical pack covering style code, intended wearer and age grade, fit and silhouette, point-of-measure diagram, size chart and grading, fabric construction and verified fiber percentages, fabric weight, color standard, seam and stitch construction, trims, labels, logo artwork and approved placement, decoration process, packaging, tolerances, and revision history.

Approve lab dips or color swatches, fabric hand-feel and composition evidence, fit samples, size-set samples where appropriate, pre-production samples and a retained reference sample. Record inspection results for measurements, seam quality, needle or sharp-object hazards, defects, color consistency, print or embroidery durability, dimensional change after laundering, colorfastness and packaging accuracy. No garment is approved from a digital mockup alone.

Apparel compliance review must assess the exact garment and intended wearer. Most covered textile and wool products require fiber-content, country-of-origin and responsible-business identification disclosures; care instructions are also required for covered products. Review the Federal Trade Commission Textile Fiber Rule and Care Labeling Rule before approving labels. General wearing-apparel textiles are subject to the applicable flammability requirements under 16 C.F.R. Part 1610, subject to its exact exemptions and test rules. Children's apparel and sleepwear require a separate product-specific assessment before sourcing or sale. Do not guess at compliance based on a supplier's generic statement.

The company identity required on a label must be the actual responsible business name or a valid Federal Trade Commission registered identification number where permitted. A clothing trademark by itself may not satisfy the responsible-business identification requirement.

## Launch sequence

**Phase 0 — Foundation:** product registry, documentation requirements, supplier records, quality gates, digital release controls and audit trail.

**Phase 1 — Low-risk pilots:** prioritize non-powered accessories and digital products that J-ROC AI can fully test and support. Obtain supplier quotes and samples only after written approval.

**Phase 2 — Powered electronics:** proceed only after exact-model compliance route, sample testing, labeling and warranty support are confirmed.

**Phase 3 — Mobility, power and off-grid systems:** require strict model compatibility, electrical safety evidence, clear installation warnings and qualified technical review.

**Phase 4 — Scale:** approved purchase orders, batch traceability, replenishment thresholds, customer support metrics, return analytics and margin reporting.

Required release controls

PPhysical products
Verified supplier and private-label rights; exact-model production sample; product-specific compliance evidence; safety labels; complete manual; warranty and returns; landed-cost calculation; inspected inventory lot.
DDigital products
J-ROC AI ownership or commercial rights; software component and license review; security and privacy review; tested installer or download; version and checksum; user documentation; license and refund terms.
SStore publication
Real images and specifications; accurate stock and fulfillment; approved price; visible manuals and support; no invented certifications, ratings or reviews.
A certification logo or safety claim may only be used when evidence and mark-use rights apply to the exact model and factory. A similar product certificate is not sufficient.

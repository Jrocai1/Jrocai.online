# Product Publication Gate

A product is not eligible for public publication until every applicable requirement below is satisfied and a Chairman-authorized approver records approval.

## Required product record
- Stable product identifier and exact manufacturer/model/variant.
- Product name, accurate description, department, and category.
- Verified manufacturer or approved supplier identity.
- Source references for specifications and claims.
- Actual product photographs or approved manufacturer assets with usage rights.
- Supplier cost, shipping cost, selling price, and margin calculation.
- Inventory source: J-ROC AI-owned or approved supplier fulfillment.
- Accurate availability and fulfillment estimate.
- Shipping, return, and warranty terms.
- Required compatibility, voltage, current, dimensions, and safety details where applicable.

## Required documentation
- Product-specific manual or manufacturer documentation.
- Setup and installation instructions where applicable.
- Operating instructions.
- Maintenance instructions.
- Troubleshooting instructions.
- Safety and regulatory information where applicable.
- Revision or model match confirmation.

Missing or conflicting documentation blocks publication. Do not invent specifications, certifications, warranties, test results, stock, reviews, or manufacturer statements.

## Certified Pre-Owned — Tested Working
Each individual unit must have:
- Unique unit or serial identifier where available.
- Actual item-specific photographs.
- Recorded inspection date and technician or process identity.
- Defined functional test checklist and results.
- Cosmetic grade: Like New, Excellent, Good, Fair, or Parts or Repair Only.
- Known defects, missing accessories, battery health where measurable, and repair history.
- Condition-specific price, warranty, and return disclosures.

Do not use the Tested Working label unless the recorded test passed. Parts or Repair Only items must never be represented as working.

## Supplier-fulfilled products
- Supplier must be approved and due diligence recorded.
- Supplier product identifier and exact variant must be verified.
- Availability, handling time, shipping origin, delivery estimate, returns, and warranty must be confirmed.
- Listing must disclose supplier fulfillment accurately.
- No outside marketplace seller listings.

## Approval and audit
Record submitter, reviewer, evidence references, decision, timestamp, product revision, and reason for rejection or approval. Publication, price changes, removal, and policy changes require Chairman approval.

# Product Sourcing Candidate Register

**Status: research leads only — not sellable inventory.**  
Research date: 2026-11-14.

This register starts the product-sourcing pipeline with manufacturer-listed products in the requested off-grid power and e-mobility departments. A manufacturer's public retail price is not a J-ROC AI cost, wholesale quote, reseller authorization, inventory count, or margin.

## Initial candidate groups

- BLUETTI portable power stations: Elite 100 V2, Elite 200 V2, Apex 200, Elite 300, Apex 300.
- Anker SOLIX home backup: S5000.
- SMLRO e-mobility replacement parts: controller and display families, pending exact model/variant confirmation.

## Required before a candidate becomes inventory

1. Confirm direct manufacturer or authorized distributor relationship and permission to resell.
2. Obtain a dated wholesale quote, minimum order quantity, shipping cost, taxes/duties, payment terms, and return/defect allowance.
3. Confirm available stock or supplier-fulfilled lead times in writing.
4. Obtain exact model and revision manuals, specifications, warranty, safety, installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting documents.
5. For electrical and battery products, verify applicable certifications, transport restrictions, installation limits, and safe handling instructions.
6. Calculate contribution margin after product cost, shipping, payment fees, expected returns, support, and fulfillment.
7. Chairman approves the exact listing, selling price, source, fulfillment method, and terms.

## Margin gate

No product is called high-margin until a real supplier quote is compared against a defensible selling price and all variable costs. Do not use manufacturer retail price as supplier cost.

## Publication gate

Do not copy these candidates into `store/catalog.json`. The public catalog remains empty until each product has a complete evidence pack and an approved publication record.
